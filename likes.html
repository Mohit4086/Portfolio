Favorite Computer Games

Euro Truck Simulator

Driving through peaceful highways and beautiful landscapes is a relaxing experience. I enjoy the feeling of being on the open road, delivering cargo across countries without any rush.

Minecraft

Building entire worlds block by block teaches creativity and patience. Every time I play, I find new ways to create structures, explore adventures, and survive the wild.

Assetto Corsa

As a racing enthusiast, Assetto Corsa gives me the closest feeling to real-life driving. The attention to detail, controls, and the thrill of perfecting each race lap keeps me hooked.

Favorite Mobile Games

PUBG

PUBG is my go-to for intense, fast-paced gaming sessions and thrilling battles. The adrenaline rush of surviving till the end and smart strategic gameplay make every match unique and exciting.

WCC3

This game lets me experience the excitement of cricket on my phone. I love building my own team, planning strategies, and leading them to victory in thrilling tournaments.

Stumble Guys

Whenever I need a break or a good laugh, I turn to Stumble Guys. It's fun, unpredictable, and the colorful chaos never fails to make me smile.

Favorite Outdoor Game & Player

Cricket

Cricket has been my favorite sport growing up. I love how it brings people together, tests patience, and rewards smart thinking and teamwork on the field.

Virat Kohli

Virat Kohli inspires me with his dedication, aggression, and commitment to excellence. Watching him play motivates me to bring the same energy and passion into my own goals.
